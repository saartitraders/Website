This notice explains, in plain words, what personal data SAARTI collects, why, who handles it, how long we keep it and what you can do about it.

Who we are

SAARTI is an app made by Saarti Traders.

Contact: support@saarti.in

What we collect and why

1. Your account

  • Your email address, your password and the one-time login codes we email you.
  • Why: to create your account, let you sign in and keep your account safe.
  • Your password is stored only in hashed form by our login service. We cannot read it. We do not keep the login codes.

2. Your profile

  • First and last name, date of birth, mobile number, PAN (optional) and a profile photo (optional).
  • Why: to set up and identify your account. We use your date of birth to confirm that you are 18 or older.
  • Your PAN is shown masked in the app (only the first 5 characters).

3. What you type in My Data

  • Credit cards: bank name, name on card, card network, only the last 4 digits of the card, credit limit, outstanding amount, due amount, minimum due, statement date, due date, interest rate and card EMIs.
  • Loans: bank or lender name, loan type, loan amount, balance, interest rate, tenure, monthly EMI, EMIs paid, EMI date and missed EMIs.
  • Payments you mark as paid: the amount and the date.
  • Bill reminders are created on your phone. They show only the bank name, never an amount.
  • Why: to show your cards and loans and to calculate your dues.
  • We never ask for your full card number, CVV, PIN, OTPs or bank passwords. Please never type them into the app.

4. Your CIBIL report PDF, only if you upload it

  • The PDF is read once by an AI service (Google Gemini) to pick out your score and your accounts.
  • The PDF is deleted from our storage right after it is read, also when reading fails. If the app is closed or loses its connection in the middle of an upload, a copy can stay in private storage until the next deletion; Delete account always removes it.
  • We keep only what was picked out: your score, the report date and, for each account, the bank, account type, amounts, interest rate, EMI, tenure, missed payments, status and the last 4 digits of the account number if they are printed.
  • Your report itself can contain your PAN and full account numbers. We do not store them, but the AI service sees them while it reads the PDF.

5. Technical logs

  • Our server functions write only counts, file sizes, status codes and error types to their logs. They never write your documents, your numbers, your name or your email.
  • Our hosting provider also keeps its own standard request logs (for example the time of a request).

We do not use your contacts, SMS, location or camera. The app asks for access to your photos only when you choose a profile photo, to your files only when you pick a CIBIL PDF, and to show notifications only if you turn on bill reminders.

Who handles your data

  • Cloud hosting: our cloud provider stores your account data (profile, cards, loans, saved CIBIL details and photo) on servers in Mumbai, India.
  • AI reading (Google Gemini): reads your CIBIL PDF only if you upload one. Google may process it outside India.
  • Email delivery: an email service sends your login codes to your email address.

We do not show ads. We do not sell your data. We do not share it with anyone else, except where the law requires us to. We try to handle your data in line with India's data protection law, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Your consent and how to withdraw it

You give your consent when you register: once for the Terms of Service and separately for this Privacy Notice. You give it again, separately, before your first CIBIL upload.

You can withdraw your consent at any time in Profile > Help and legal > Withdraw consent and delete data. This deletes your account and your data.

How long we keep your data

  • We keep your data while your account exists.
  • Reset All Data removes your cards, loans, card EMIs, payment records and saved CIBIL details, and keeps your account.
  • Delete account removes your profile, photo, cards, loans, card EMIs, payment records, saved CIBIL details and files at once.
  • We do not keep separate backups of your data.

Your rights

  • See and correct your data: use Edit profile and My Data.
  • Erase your data: use Delete account.
  • Withdraw your consent: see above.
  • Get a copy of your data: tap Download my data in your Profile to get it as a PDF, Excel or JSON file.
  • Nominate someone: you can name another person to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act. Write to us to do this.
  • Complain: write to our Grievance Officer.

Grievance Officer: Sahil Kumar

Email: grievance@saarti.in

We reply within 30 days. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

  • Data is encrypted when it travels between the app and our servers (HTTPS) and when it is stored by our cloud provider.
  • Each user can read and change only their own records. The database enforces this.
  • Your profile photo and CIBIL PDF are kept in private storage. Other users cannot open them.
  • Your CIBIL PDF is deleted after it is read.
  • Your login session is saved on your phone so that you stay signed in. Sign out on a phone you share.

Children

SAARTI is only for people aged 18 and over. The app checks your date of birth.

Changes to this notice

The version and date are shown at the top. If we change this notice in a way that matters, we will ask for your consent again.

SAARTIA Product by SAARTI TRADERS

SAARTI is an information and tracking tool. It is not a bank or lender, does not give loans, and is not linked to CIBIL/TransUnion or any bank. It does not give financial advice.
