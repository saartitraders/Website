This Privacy Policy explains, in simple words, what personal data SAARTI collects, why we need it, where it is kept, who handles it and what you can do about it. We handle your data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act").

1. Who we are

SAARTI is a product of Saarti Traders.

Saarti Traders decides why and how your data is used. Under the DPDP Act, this makes us the "Data Fiduciary".

2. What we collect and why

Your account

  • Your email address and password. If you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your email address and your name with us, and you do not need a password.
  • Your password is kept only in a scrambled (hashed) form by our login service. We cannot read it.
  • One-time codes we email you (to reset your password or to confirm account deletion) are checked by our login service. We do not keep them.
  • Why: to create your account and let you sign in safely.

Your profile

  • First and last name, date of birth, mobile number, PAN (optional) and a profile photo (optional).
  • Why: to set up your account and to confirm that you are 18 or older. Your name, date of birth, mobile number and PAN are also used on your phone to try to open a password-locked CIBIL PDF (see section 3).
  • Your PAN, mobile number and date of birth are encrypted before they are stored (see section 5). Your PAN is shown masked in the app.

What you type in My Data

  • Credit cards: bank name, name on card, card network, only the last 4 digits of the card, credit limit, outstanding amount, due amount, minimum due, statement date, due date, interest rate and card EMIs.
  • Bank loans: lender name, loan type, loan amount, balance, interest rate, tenure, EMI, EMIs paid, due date and missed EMIs.
  • Loans from friends, family or a local financer: the same, plus the name you give the lender, how you repay (daily, weekly or monthly), the cash you actually received, late fees, interest added, and any security you gave (for example "Gold chain") with its value.
  • Payments you record, and each month's bill and statement amounts.
  • Why: to show your debts in one place, work out your dues and remind you on time.
  • We never ask for your full card number, CVV, PIN, OTP or net-banking password. Please never type them into the app.

Feedback

  • If you send feedback: the type, your rating, your message, the app version and whether your phone is Android or iOS. It is stored on our servers with your account.

Phone permissions

  • We do not read your contacts, SMS, location or camera.
  • Profile photo and CIBIL PDF: you pick them with your phone's own picker. The app does not get access to your whole gallery or files.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on bill reminders.
  • Fingerprint or face: only if you turn on app lock. Your phone checks it; we never receive it.

3. Your CIBIL report and AI reading

You upload your CIBIL report only if you choose to. We ask for your separate consent before your first upload.

  • Password-locked PDF: it is opened on your phone. The app first tries the usual password formats made from your own profile details (name, date of birth, mobile number, PAN), then asks you to type the password. The password is never saved and never sent to us.
  • Reading: the opened PDF is uploaded to private storage. Our server function sends it once to Google Gemini, an AI service, which picks out your score and your accounts.
  • Deleting the PDF: the PDF is deleted right after it is read, also when reading fails or you cancel. If the upload is cut off in the middle (for example the app is closed or the internet drops), a copy can stay in private storage. Delete account always removes it.
  • What we keep: your score, the report date, the score factors and, for each account, the lender, account type, amounts, credit limit, interest rate, EMI, tenure, missed payments, status, closing date and the last 4 digits of the account number when the report prints them in full. The account details and score factors are stored encrypted.
  • What we do not keep: your report also has your PAN, address, full account numbers and other details. We do not store them, but Google Gemini sees the whole PDF while it reads it. Google may process it outside India.
  • AI can make mistakes. Check the figures against your report before you rely on them.
  • On your phone the app also keeps a short score history: only the report date and the score.

4. CIBIL disputes

  • Your dispute list (which accounts, the reason, a short note and the Control Number you type) is kept only on your phone.
  • The complaint letter is made on your phone and opened in your own email app. You decide whether to send it and to whom.
  • SAARTI sends nothing to TransUnion CIBIL, to any bank or to any lender.

5. How we protect your data

  • All data travels between the app and our servers over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Our cloud provider encrypts all stored data.
  • On top of that, your PAN, mobile number, date of birth and CIBIL account details are encrypted by our server (AES-256) before they are stored. The key is kept as a secret of our server functions. It is never in the database or in the app.
  • Each user can read and change only their own records. The database enforces this.
  • Your profile photo and CIBIL PDF are kept in private storage that other users cannot open.
  • Our server functions write only counts, file sizes, status codes and error types to their logs. They never write your documents, numbers, name or email.
  • Your login session is saved on your phone (not encrypted by the app) so that you stay signed in. On a shared phone, sign out or turn on app lock.

6. What stays only on your phone

  • Bill reminders: made and scheduled on your phone, with no server. They never show an amount or a bank or lender name, only text like "A bill is due tomorrow".
  • App lock: your PIN is kept on your phone only, as a salted hash in the phone's secure storage. Fingerprint and face are checked by your phone. Neither is ever sent to us.
  • Your dispute list, Control Number, score history, card colours, Compare tab answers and the tips you have hidden.
  • The app does not keep an offline copy of your cards and loans. They are loaded from our servers and held only while the app is open.
  • Files you make with Download my data are created on your phone and shared only through the app you choose. Temporary copies are cleared later.
  • Sign out removes your reminders and temporary files from the phone. Delete account removes everything the app kept for you on that phone.

7. Crash reports

  • The released app sends crash reports to Sentry, a crash-reporting service with servers in Germany (EU).
  • A report holds the technical error, the names of the last app screens you opened, the app version and the phone platform.
  • Before a report leaves your phone, the app removes email addresses, PAN, mobile numbers, amounts, login tokens, server addresses and your account identity. No screenshots are taken and no taps or typing are recorded.

8. Who handles your data

We share your data only with service providers who process it for us (under the DPDP Act, "Data Processors"):

  • Supabase: database, login, file storage and server functions. Our servers are in Mumbai, India.
  • Google: Gemini reads your CIBIL PDF (only if you upload one). Google Sign-in (only if you use it).
  • Sentry: crash reports (servers in Germany, EU).
  • An email delivery service: sends your one-time codes.
  • Expo: we use Expo's tools to build the app. The app does not send your data to Expo.

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. The app has no advertising or analytics trackers. We share data with others only when the law requires it.

9. How long we keep your data

  • While your account exists.
  • Reset All Data (in Profile): removes your cards, loans, card EMIs, payment records and saved CIBIL report straight away. Your account and profile stay.
  • Delete account: removes your login, profile, photo, cards, loans, card EMIs, payment records, saved CIBIL report, feedback and uploaded files straight away, and the app's data for you on that phone.
  • We do not keep separate backups of your data.
  • Crash reports are kept by Sentry for a limited period and then deleted.

10. Your rights and how to use them

Under the DPDP Act you have these rights:

  • See your data: Profile > Download my data gives you a copy as a PDF, Excel or JSON file.
  • Correct your data: use Edit profile and My Data.
  • Erase your data: use Reset All Data, or Delete account. To delete your account you confirm with your password, or with a code we email you if you signed in with Google.
  • Withdraw your consent: Profile > Help and legal > Withdraw consent and delete data. This deletes your account and data, because the app cannot work without them. It does not affect what was done before you withdrew.
  • Nominate someone: you can name a person to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act. Write to our Grievance Officer to do this.
  • Complain: write to our Grievance Officer.
