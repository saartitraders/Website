What SAARTI is

SAARTI is a tool to track your credit cards, loans, EMIs and dues, and to read your own CIBIL report.

SAARTI is not a bank, not a lender and not TransUnion CIBIL. It is not a licensed financial advisor. What you see in the app is for your own tracking. It is not financial, legal or tax advice.

Who can use it

You must be 18 or older and give accurate details about yourself.

Your data and your duties

  • Enter or upload only your own information and documents.
  • Keep your email and password safe. Tell us if you think someone else is using your account.
  • The app is only as correct as what you enter. Keep your details up to date.

AI reading of documents

  • When you upload a CIBIL report, an AI service reads it. The result can contain mistakes. Check it before you rely on it.
  • Figures the app calculates, such as the calculated due and EMI estimates, are estimates. They can differ from your bank's statement. Your bank's figure always wins.
