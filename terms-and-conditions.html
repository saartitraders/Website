1. The service

SAARTI – Debt Free helps you organise your credit cards, loans, EMIs and due dates; track payments; view a credit report you upload; compare that report with your own records; and explore debt-planning ideas such as repayment strategies and consolidation. Features, screens and availability may change as the app develops.

2. Who can use SAARTI

You must be at least 18 years old and able to enter a legally binding contract in India. You must use SAARTI only for your own finances, or for someone else’s only with their clear permission.

Strategies, tips, estimates and consolidation suggestions are general, are based only on the data you enter, and are not personalised financial, legal or tax advice. We do not guarantee that you will become debt free, reduce interest, improve your credit score, or be approved for any loan. Please consider speaking to a qualified professional before making major financial decisions.

6. Credit reports

Credit report data can be delayed, incomplete or different from your own records. Comparison results are for information only. SAARTI does not change credit bureau records, raise disputes on your behalf or contact lenders for you. If you find an error in your report, raise it with the credit bureau or your lender directly.

Marking a bill or EMI as paid in SAARTI only updates your own record. It does not make a payment or transfer money. You remain responsible for paying your bills on time and for any late fees, interest or credit-score effects of missed payments.
