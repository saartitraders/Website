1. What SAARTI is

SAARTI is a tracking and information tool. It helps you keep track of your credit cards, loans, EMIs and dues, and read your own CIBIL report.

SAARTI is not a lender and not a bank. It is not a credit bureau. It is not part of, or linked to, TransUnion CIBIL. It does not give financial, legal or tax advice, and it is not a licensed financial adviser.

2. Who can use it

You must be 18 or older. Give true details about yourself.

3. Your data and your duties

  • You are responsible for the data you enter or upload. Enter or upload only your own information and documents.
  • The app is only as correct as what you enter. Keep your details up to date.
  • Keep your password safe. Tell us at once if you think someone else is using your account.

4. Figures and AI reading

  • Dues, interest, EMIs, payoff plans and other figures in the app are estimates. They can differ from your bank's or lender's statement. Your bank's or lender's figure always wins.
  • When you upload a CIBIL report, an AI service reads it. The result can contain mistakes. Check it against your report before you rely on it.
  • Reminders depend on your phone, which can delay or block them. Always check your due dates yourself.

5. CIBIL dispute letters

The dispute letter in the app is only a draft. You read it, change it if needed and send it yourself from your own email. SAARTI does not send it, does not file disputes for you and does not act for you with TransUnion CIBIL, any bank or any lender.

6. Acceptable use

  • Use the app only for lawful purposes.
  • Do not upload anyone else's report or details without their permission.
  • Do not try to break, overload, copy or get around the security of the app or our servers.
  • Do not use the app to harm, cheat or harass anyone.

7. Changes to the service

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in Profile > Danger zone > Delete account. Your data is then deleted as described in our Privacy Policy. We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms.
