You can delete your SAARTI account and all your data at any time. This page explains how.

In the app

  • Open SAARTI and sign in.
  • On Home, tap your profile picture at the top right.
  • Tap Help and legal > Withdraw consent and delete data, or scroll down to Danger zone > Delete account.
  • Type DELETE in capital letters, enter your current password and tap Delete my account.

This works at any time and takes effect at once. You need an internet connection. You are signed out and cannot sign in with this account again.

What is deleted

  • Your login account.
  • Your profile and profile photo.
  • Your cards, loans and card EMIs.
  • Your saved CIBIL details and any uploaded files.

If you cannot open the app

Write from your registered email address to support@saarti.in with the subject "Delete my SAARTI account". We may ask you one question to confirm that it is you. We delete your account and data within 30 days.

Keep your account but remove your data

In the same Profile screen, tap Danger zone > Reset All Data. This removes your cards, loans, card EMIs, payment records and saved CIBIL details, and keeps your account.

Backups

We do not keep separate backups of your data.
