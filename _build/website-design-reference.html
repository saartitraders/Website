CIBIL report

Check your report against your own records

Upload your CIBIL report PDF

Download your report from the CIBIL website and upload the PDF in the CIBIL tab. AI reads your score, cards and loans from it. The PDF is deleted right after it is read; only the details picked out are kept.

You choose to upload. SAARTI does not connect to your bank or to CIBIL.

See what does not match

Compare puts the accounts on your CIBIL report next to the ones you typed in. Mark each row OK or Wrong, and flag accounts that are not yours.

Comparing does not change your CIBIL report.

Raise a dispute on the CIBIL website

If something on your report is wrong, the app shows you how to raise a dispute with CIBIL yourself, and keeps your own records correct in the meantime.

SAARTI is not linked to CIBIL or TransUnion.

Help & FAQ

Questions people ask

All questions
Is my data safe?

Your data is saved in your SAARTI account with our cloud provider. Each user can see and change only their own records. We never ask for your full card number, CVV, PIN or bank passwords.

Does SAARTI read my SMS, bank account or contacts?

No. SAARTI does not read your SMS, contacts or location, and it does not connect to your bank account. Everything in My Data is what you type in.

How do I get my CIBIL report PDF?

Download your report as a PDF from the CIBIL website, then open the CIBIL tab and tap Upload CIBIL Report (PDF).

What happens to my PDF after I upload it?

It is read by an AI service to pick out your score, cards and loans, and deleted right after. We keep only the details it picked out.

How is "Due This Month" calculated?

It adds this month's due for every card and the EMI of every open loan in My Data, and takes off what you marked as paid.

How do bill reminders work?

Reminders are set on your phone 3 days before, 1 day before and on each due date, if you turn them on and allow notifications.
