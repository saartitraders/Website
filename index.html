Contact & support
What is SAARTI – Debt Free?+

SAARTI is an upcoming Android app for organizing cards, loans, EMIs and payment records. It also helps you review differences between credit-report information and your manually entered records.

Can I add my cards and loans manually?+

Yes. The My Data section includes manual card, loan and EMI records. The app screens show options to add and manage these details.

Does SAARTI make payments or correct my CIBIL report?+

No. Marking a payment only updates your tracking record; it is not a bank payment. Comparing a report does not automatically change credit bureau records or file disputes.

Will it guarantee debt freedom or improve my credit score?+

No. SAARTI helps organize and review information. It does not guarantee debt freedom, credit score improvement, savings or loan approval.

When can I download the app?+

SAARTI is coming soon. An official Google Play link will be added here once the app is released. A launch date and premium pricing have not been announced.

Is my data safe, and who can see it?+

Your records belong to you. We do not sell your data, and we use safeguards such as access controls and encrypted connections. PAN and credit report upload are optional. Read the full details in the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account and data?+

You can delete your account from the app’s Help & FAQ section or by emailing us. You can also clear only your entries with Reset All My Data. See the Account Deletion page for steps.

Is SAARTI free?+

Core tracking features are planned to be available to everyone. Some planning features are marked as premium. Pricing has not been announced and will be shown in the app before you pay.
